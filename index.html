Booking Rules

Follow these every time — no exceptions.

  1. Book 3 days in advance. No same-day or next-day bookings.
  2. Book-in window is 07:00 – 10:00 on weekdays only.
  3. No afternoon drop-offs. Late arrivals can't be accepted.
  4. No Saturday bookings. Workshop and booking desk are closed.
  5. Saturday → Monday: Central can assist with Monday bookings when a client calls on Saturday.
  6. Foton requests: take a message and pass it to the Booking Clerk — don't book.
  7. Confirmations go to: Sichulumancile MATYOLO, Naledi Mayekiso, Yaleka Dinana, Lwazi Nenemba.
  8. Always CC the manager on escalations and bookings.
