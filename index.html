03

Booking Rules

Non-negotiable operating procedures

MANDATORY
▣Booking Protocol [08 RULES]
  1. Book 3 days in advance. No same-day or next-day bookings.
  2. Book-in window 07:00 – 10:00 on weekdays only.
  3. No afternoon drop-offs. Late arrivals cannot be accepted.
  4. No Saturday bookings. Workshop and desk are closed.
  5. Saturday → Monday: Central can assist with Monday bookings.
  6. Foton requests: take a message, pass to Booking Clerk — do not book.
  7. Confirmations to: Sichulumancile MATYOLO, Naledi Mayekiso, Yaleka Dinana, Lwazi Nenemba.
  8. Always CC the manager on escalations and bookings.
